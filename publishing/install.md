# Installing Synergy

Steps to send the user. Use whichever route is live.

## From the Chrome Web Store (once approved)

1. Open the link we send you in Chrome.
2. Click **Add to Chrome**, then **Add extension**.
3. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome and pin **Synergy**, so it stays in your toolbar.
4. Click the Synergy icon. Today's leads open in a panel on the right of the page you're on.

Chrome keeps it up to date automatically.

## Direct download (while the store review runs)

1. Download the ZIP file we send you and double-click it to unzip it. Keep the folder somewhere it won't be deleted, such as Documents.
2. In Chrome, go to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped folder.
5. Pin Synergy from the puzzle-piece icon, then click it. Today's leads open in a panel on the right.

Chrome may show a banner about developer-mode extensions. That's expected for this route. When the store version is ready, remove this copy from `chrome://extensions` and install from the store link instead.
